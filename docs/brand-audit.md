# Camp Coleman Brand Audit

Status: DRAFT — Phase 0
Prepared for: Coleman Storybook (working name)
Date: 2026-08-08
Source: Public inspection of https://campcoleman.org/ (homepage, /alumni, /about-us) via automated fetch. No official Camp Coleman brand guide, logo package, or asset library was supplied as of this writing.

This document strictly separates:
- **OBSERVED** — directly seen on the public site or in supplied materials.
- **INFERRED** — a reasonable extrapolation from OBSERVED material, not confirmed.
- **RECOMMENDED** — a design/product recommendation for Coleman Storybook, not a claim about Camp Coleman's official standards.

Nothing in this document should be presented to Camp Coleman staff, board, or funders as their "official" brand standard. See `docs/legal-review-required.md` and the Owner Input Required note at the end of this file.

## 1. Visual Identity

**OBSERVED**
- The site displays a Camp Coleman logo/emblem consistently across pages, including as a favicon.
- Overall site aesthetic reads as clean and modern rather than rustic/vintage.
- Navigation is organized around two audiences at once: prospective families ("Thinking about camp?", "Ready to sign up!") and existing community ("Alumni", "Camp Store", "Blog", "Donate").
- A countdown timer to opening day appears on the homepage, used as an anticipation/urgency device.

**INFERRED**
- No specific hex color values, font families, or spacing rules could be reliably extracted via automated page-content inspection (the fetch tool converts pages to text/markdown and does not expose computed CSS). Actual brand colors and typography are therefore UNKNOWN pending either (a) Coleman supplying a brand guide, or (b) a manual/visual review of the live site by a human or a screenshot-based audit.
- Given URJ camp-network conventions and the outdoor/Georgia mountain setting, it is plausible the palette leans on natural greens/blues with a warm accent, but this is a guess, not a finding.

**Separately, and NOT to be confused with Camp Coleman's actual brand:** an earlier, unrelated deliverable in this project workspace (the "GET A HOLD" Founders Day 2026 fundraising deck) used a placeholder palette — Navy `#121E33`, Navy Deep `#0A1220`, Forest `#1E3E32`, Coral `#E06A3E`, White, Light BG `#F4F5F3`, Ink `#1A1A1A` — that was explicitly documented as an INFERRED/placeholder system pending Coleman's real brand guide. That deck's own notes say Coleman's official brand guide, once supplied, "should supersede the inferred color/typography choices." Coleman Storybook should treat that palette the same way: a candidate starting point, not a source of truth.

**RECOMMENDED**
- Do not hard-code any specific hex values as "Camp Coleman brand colors" in application code. Implement brand tokens as a configurable `OrganizationBrand` layer (see `docs/architecture.md`) seeded initially with a clearly-labeled placeholder palette, swappable the moment Coleman supplies real guidelines or a marketing contact confirms colors from the live site design.
- Before Phase 2 visual design work goes further than placeholder tokens, obtain (from Brian or another Coleman stakeholder) either: a brand guide PDF, Canva/brand-kit link, or explicit sign-off on colors sampled from the live site.

## 3. Messaging Themes (OBSERVED, from homepage / about-us / alumni pages)

- **Mission statement:** "Inspire campers and staff to be caring, committed, and connected to the Jewish community through an extraordinary camp experience."
- **Four core Jewish values named on the site:** Chesed (kindness), Kavod (respect), Kehillah (community), Shalom (peace).
- **Tagline-like self-description:** "enriching Jewish lives since 1962."
- **History:** Founded 1962 as the SEFTY Camp Institute near Cleveland, Georgia; renamed Camp Coleman in 1964 after supporters Ray and Philip Coleman; grew from dozens to nearly 700 campers by 1967 under director Allan F. Solomon.
- **Philosophy language:** a "tech-free, parent-free environment"; campers "live a wholly Jewish life while at camp"; the aim to build "joyful, caring, and meaningful Reform Jewish community."
- **Testimonial language actually used on the homepage:** a parent saying their child "developed life skills and a joy for his identity"; another crediting camp with boosting their son's "self-confidence." (These are Coleman's own existing testimonial excerpts, not ours — do not reuse verbatim as if generated by Coleman Storybook without attribution/permission.)
- **Alumni page voice:** "We know that our alumni love camp, and we love you!"; "It doesn't matter where in the world you live, how much time you have to give"; solicits "photos and videos from summers past."
- **Financial accessibility framing:** language about being "committed to helping make the Coleman experience available" (financial assistance).
- **CTAs observed:** "REQUEST INFO," "Register Now," "LEARN MORE," "Watch our videos," "Donate Now!," "Take the Alumni Census," "Share with us."
- **Tone:** warm, inclusive, aspirational; balances institutional authority (mission, values, history) with personal/testimonial voice; intergenerational framing recurs (alumni, "generation to generation" resonates with the mission's continuity theme even though that exact phrase was not found verbatim on the site).

## 4. Assets Requiring Explicit Permission

**DO NOT** scrape and permanently rehost the following without Coleman's explicit permission, even though they are publicly visible:
- Any photograph of campers, staff, or families appearing on campcoleman.org, its social channels, or in the alumni/testimonial sections.
- The Camp Coleman logo/emblem file itself (use only an official file supplied by Coleman, or a text/wordmark placeholder in the interim).
- Any existing testimonial video or quote — these belong to the contributors who gave them and to Coleman's existing consent/release process, which is very likely separate from Coleman Storybook's own consent system.

**RECOMMENDED for V1:** ship with clearly-labeled placeholder imagery (solid color/gradient blocks, generic outdoor/camp-adjacent stock only if properly licensed, or simple illustrated motifs) and a placeholder wordmark, with an explicit `docs/legal-review-required.md` item asking Coleman to supply an approved photo/logo package before any real public launch.

## 5. Motifs Worth Considering (RECOMMENDED, not confirmed brand standards)

Georgia mountain/outdoor setting, tree-line silhouettes, simple topographic or trail-line shapes, warm campfire-adjacent tones for emotional moments (e.g., the consent/thank-you screens), and understated Jewish identity cues (e.g., a subtle Star of David or Hebrew accent used sparingly and respectfully, never decoratively or as a generic "Judaica" cliché). Any use of Hebrew text or religious iconography should be reviewed by Coleman staff for appropriateness and accuracy before shipping.
